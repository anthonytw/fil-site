fil

Reprendre le fil: to pick up the thread where you left off.

A remote shell for development over SSH that keeps your place.

  • Sessions that survive reconnects, roaming and sleep, with your terminal's own scrollback.
  • Reattach after a crash, or take a session over from another machine.
  • Port forwards that stay open across reconnects.
  • Direct QUIC, or a plain SSH pipe when UDP is blocked.
